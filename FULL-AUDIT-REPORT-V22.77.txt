3. Upload all files from this ZIP together to the GitHub Pages project root.
4. Open the deployed CRM online once to activate the new Service Worker.
5. Test using a non-production/test account first, including inventory archive, stock movements, service completion, invoices, AMC and two-device sync.
6. Do not test destructive actions on the only copy of live business data.
